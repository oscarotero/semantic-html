# Carousel

## Auto-Rotating Image Carousel Example

```html
<section aria-roledescription="carousel"
  aria-label="Description of the carousel">
  <div aria-live="off" aria-live="polite" id="carousel-items">
    <div aria-role="group" aria-roledescription="slide"
      aria-label="1 of 4">...</div>
    <div aria-role="group" aria-roledescription="slide"
      aria-label="2 of 4">...</div>
    <div aria-role="group" aria-roledescription="slide"
      aria-label="3 of 4">...</div>
    <div aria-role="group" aria-roledescription="slide"
      aria-label="4 of 4">...</div>
  </div>

  <button aria-controls="carousel-items">Play/pause</button>
</section>
```
